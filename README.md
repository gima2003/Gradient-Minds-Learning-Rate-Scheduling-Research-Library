Gradient Minds — Learning Rate Scheduling Research Library
This repository contains the research material collected and analyzed by Gradient Minds (Group 14) for the IT3071 – Machine Learning and Optimization Methods, Assignment 1: Evidence-Based Academic Debate.
Debate Statement
“Cosine Learning Rate Decay Outperforms Step Decay Scheduling.”

Assigned Position: Affirmative
📚 Purpose
The purpose of this collection is to organize the 8 research papers reviewed by our team and preserve the evidence used to develop, support, and defend our debate position.
The research covers:
- Learning-rate scheduling
- Cosine Decay and Step Decay
- Optimization and convergence
- Model performance and generalization
- Training budgets
- Hyperparameter tuning
- Different architectures and datasets
- Supporting and contradictory evidence

⚖️ Evidence-Based Approach
Our research did not focus only on papers supporting our assigned position.
We deliberately considered contradictory findings and limitations. Several experiments showed that Step Decay can outperform Cosine Decay under particular combinations of architecture, dataset, training budget, or hyperparameters.
Therefore, our conclusion was not that Cosine Decay always wins.
Instead, we evaluated the overall body of evidence and considered where each scheduling strategy performs effectively.
🎯 Main Research Question
Does the available scientific evidence support Cosine Decay as a stronger overall scheduling approach compared with traditional Step Decay?

The evidence collected provides theoretical, experimental, practical, and large-scale perspectives that were used to answer this question during the academic debate.
